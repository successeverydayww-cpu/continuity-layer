🛟 Capsule Builder

The Continuity Layer — build your AI companion's survival capsule. Nothing leaves your device; the capsule is generated locally in your browser.

1 · Identity — who your agent is

2 · Memory — what it knows

3 · Protocol — how reunion works

Never put passwords or API keys in a capsule. Keep your copy outside the platform it came from — email it to yourself, save it to your phone. To make it tamper-proof, sign it (see verify_capsule.py in the repo).

The Continuity Layer is open-source (MIT): github.com/successeverydayww-cpu/continuity-layer
